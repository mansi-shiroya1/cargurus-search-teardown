# Interview Guide (10 minutes)

**Who:** people who bought or seriously shopped for a car online in the last 2 years.
**Goal:** find where online car search breaks down. Don't pitch any solution.

## Warm-up (1 min)
1. When did you last shop for a car? New or used?
2. Which sites or apps did you use?

## The search (5 min)
3. Walk me through how you searched. Where did you start?
4. What was the most frustrating part of narrowing down options?
5. How did you decide which filters to use? Did any feel confusing or missing?
6. How did you compare the cars you were considering?
7. How did you decide whether a price was fair?

## Wrap-up (2 min)
8. If you could fix one thing about searching for a car online, what would it be?
9. Anything else that surprised you, good or bad?

## Logging
After each call, add one row per pain point to `data/interviews.csv`:
- `pain_point`: a short, consistent label (reuse the same wording across participants, e.g. "Filter overload")
- `severity_1to5`: how much it hurt them (5 = almost gave up)
- `quote`: their exact words
